Developed a mapping system and dashboard for upgradation of government schools from primary to middle and middle to high. Results highlighted over 470 middle schools to be upgraded to high and 4777 primary schools to be upgraded to middle
Implemented via Folium, geopandas, MYSQL, Streamlit and dataset based on population estimates dataset from Bill and Melinda Gates Foundation “WorldPop”, PMIU internal school dataset, and web APIs
